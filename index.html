Why degraded is the default

A grail brainstem is not running here unless you burrowed one, and burrowing takes extra permission on the device. So most visits land in degraded mode — that is the normal resting state of this page, not a failure. When a brainstem is burrowed, this page hands you through to it.

Burrow one

# installs and starts the grail brainstem on this device
curl -fsSL https://kody-w.github.io/rapp-installer/install.sh | bash

# then come back here, or go straight to
open http://localhost:7071/

What this page will not do

It never asks for your X-Brainstem-Secret, never proxies your brainstem, and never reads a byte of a response from it. The probe is opaque on purpose: it can tell that something answered, and nothing else. Your brainstem refuses cross-origin browser calls — that guard is what stops any website from scripting it inside your browser, and this page is a website. So instead of asking it to make an exception, the page just hands you the door and gets out of the way. On the other side of that door, the same bones are served by your brainstem itself, same-origin, with the full GOD layer live and no exception made for anyone.
